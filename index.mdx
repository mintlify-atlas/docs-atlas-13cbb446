Itemzflow is an open-source homebrew application for the PlayStation 4 that replaces the default home menu with a fully-featured game manager. It lets you install packages, dump games to USB, apply game patches, mount network shares, and track playtime — all without leaving the app.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Itemzflow">
    Grab the latest PKG from [pkg-zone.com](https://pkg-zone.com/details/ITEM00001) or the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/LightningMods/Itemzflow/releases).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install on your PS4">
    Use your existing homebrew installer to install the PKG. On first launch, Itemzflow installs its background daemon automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings">
    Optionally create a `settings.ini` on your USB drive or in the app directory to configure dump paths, sort order, custom fonts, and more. See [Settings](/customization/settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your library">
    Launch Itemzflow. Your installed games appear as a scrollable grid with cover art. Use the D-Pad or analog stick to navigate, and press **Cross** to launch a game.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Itemzflow requires a jailbroken PS4. It will not function on stock firmware.
</Note>
